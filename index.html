About Me

I am Chao Wang (王超) at Nanjing University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on remote sensing object detection, knowledge distillation, SAR image understanding, and optical-SAR multimodal learning.

SAR Object Detection: Detection methods and benchmarks for SAR imagery under complex scenes.

Optical-SAR Fusion Detection: Cross-modal localization and multimodal representation learning for Earth observation.

Knowledge Distillation: Compact, accurate detectors through localization-aware and generative distillation.

Foundation Model Adaptation: Efficient adaptation of large visual models for remote sensing detection.
